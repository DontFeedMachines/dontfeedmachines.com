# Tactics & Tools

to starve the machines, we must employ both technical, social, and sometimes generally highly experimental tactics.

## The Little Things
1. `robots.txt`: explicitly ban AI crawlers like CCBot and GPTBot.
2. **nightshade/glaze**: poison your images before uploading them.
3. **paywalls**: hide your most valuable content behind human-verification.

## Social Action
- provide alternative tools for those who might gravitate towards AI due to lack of resources & access.
- demand attribution & refuse the idea that this is how things will be now.
- educate your peers on why human creation matters & why generative tools can harm.
