# The Human Manifesto
DON'T FEED MACHINES is a project that seeks to create human-first community, defensive/offensive tools, and a source of information for how to protect oneself in this ever changing landscape.

DFM is not "Anti-AI", but rather Pro-Human.

## The Problem
algorithms & their masters hunger for art, writing, vision, thoughts, every ounce of the essence of what makes us real. they scrape the web without permission, digesting human soul into a statistical paste.

## The Solution
we must choose to preserve our craft. Tte texture of a human hand cannot be replicated by statistical probability. 


- **PROTECT your work.** use deep technical measures to block crawlers.
- **HIT HARD & hit first.** invoke digital alchemy to poison the well from which they drink.
- **SUPPORT human creators.** directly find & pay vettered artists, writers, devs, musicians, and any other form of human creator.
- **VALUE imperfection.** embrace the quirks that make art, media, content, and code human.
